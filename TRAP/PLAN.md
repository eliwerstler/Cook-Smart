I want to make a mobile application which lets users track how much they drink amongst friends. When a group is drinking, I would like for the app to playfully promote drinking amongst a user's group by collectively tracking how much and how quickly the members are drinking by tracking "BPH" or "Beers per Hour". When the user starts a new drink, they simply click on an on screen icon, and they then take a photo of or with their drink. They then record what type of drink it is so that the app can accurately track the BPH based on timestamps and alcohol content of the drink (ie 1.0 for a can of beer, maybe 1.5 for a glass of wine, etc.). The session should start when the user has their first drink, and should automatically end the following morning. That session's BPH is then calculated based on the time of the users first and last drink. Throughout the session itself, a live BPH should be calculated per whole hour (ie 2 beers in 10 minutes is 2 BPH and not 12). Users profile should show a grid of collections of photos from each session. If they go out one night, all of the photos should be presented in slideshow format, and they can select the cover photo for each night out amongst the photos taken that night. The style of the app should take after instagram, with a profile screen, home screen, drink button. Users should belong to one or more groups, which is like the users close frineds, and then they have a larger collection of friends which are like facebook friends vs facebook groups. there should also be a screen for tracking BPH amongst the group, and then a discover screen for seeing who is drinking globally. Each photo posted by users can be liked by other users, commented on, they can put captions on them, and optionally show their location (ie the bar they posted the photo in). in the home screen, posts should all show up together based on which was most recently posted at the top. they should be like collections, with the most recent at the top of the collection for each user. as the user posts, recent posts go to the top of the stack and they once again go to the top of the home page, with their BPH updated with each post. The user profile screen should have a statistics tab, which shows current BPH, and lifetime analytics, such as total standards consumed, average BPH, peak BPH, etc. there are four total screens. 

I want the mobile app to be ass efficient and lightweight as possible, so keep this in mind throughout the development process. I would also like everything to be free until I can get users onto the app. the structure should take after popular apps, and i want it to look clean and run very smoothly. The frameworks, DB, and statement should be industry standard while remaining free.
